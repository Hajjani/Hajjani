## Introduction

Curiosity and bold ambition have driven humanity to incredible achievements, including the invention of C. In a world where big tech companies dominate the technology landscape, some developers still pursue their own ideas and bring them to life.

I’m a young developer passionate about C, optimization, reinforcement learning (RL), computer vision (CV), and the bloated Python. I believe that with enough dedication, we can bring meaningful ideas into the world and create things that matter. The Linux kernel is one example of what a strong vision can inspire, and the GNU Project shows how many developers can contribute to a shared effort.

## Background

I’m a Moroccan student who enjoys mathematics and programming, and I’m the main maintainer of [AllTool Linux](https://github.com/Hajjani/AllTool-Linux). I have ideas I’d like to share with the world. Even as AI reshapes programming, I believe there’s still room for curious, creative developers to make a difference.

## Resources for new developers

If you’re just getting started, these resources may be useful:

- [Beej’s Guide to Network Programming](https://beej.us/guide/bgnet/pdf/bgnet_usl_c_1.pdf) — C sockets
- [Optimizing C](https://icps.u-strasbg.fr/~bastoul/local_copies/lee.html) — C optimization
- [Introduction to Reinforcement Learning](https://web.stanford.edu/class/archive/cs/cs234/cs234.1224/slides/lecture1.pdf) — RL basics
